Measurement limits
These constraints are part of the result, not footnotes to ignore.

      

        
Client timing
TTFT and total latency include local request/runtime overhead. Prompt and generation throughput use Ollama-reported counts and durations.

        
System and GPU
CPU/RAM and NVIDIA GPU/VRAM/power/temperature are system- or device-wide samples, not isolated model consumption. Sampled peaks can miss brief spikes.

        
Energy
GPU joules are estimated by integrating whole-device power samples during the request, without subtracting idle power. This is not model-isolated or whole-laptop energy; short requests may have no estimate.

        
Quality
Phrase assertions and normalized text agreement do not measure semantic similarity or establish a hallucination rate. Starter cases are not a validated benchmark dataset.
